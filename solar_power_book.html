Key Formulas:
Efficiency: η = Pout / Pin (the fraction of incoming solar power the panel delivers as useful electricity)
Incoming solar power: Pin = (Solar Intensity) × (Area) — standard conditions: 1000 W/m²
Energy and Power: E = P × t (energy in kW·h when power is in kW and time is in hours)
Remember: Keep units consistent. Convert efficiency percentages to decimals (e.g., 20.5% → 0.205) before multiplying.
