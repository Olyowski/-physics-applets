Key Formulas:
Energy per fission (MeV): ΔE = Σ(BEproducts) − Σ(BEreactant) — multiply each binding energy per nucleon by that nucleus's mass number A; free neutrons contribute ≈ 0.
MeV → Joules: 1 MeV = 1.602 × 10⁻¹³ J
Atoms per gram of 235U: NA / 235 = 6.022 × 10²³ / 235 ≈ 2.563 × 10²¹ atoms/g
Energy per gram (kJ/g): (J/atom) × (atoms/g) ÷ 1000
Remember: Compare to natural gas at 54.0 kJ/g. Ratio = (kJ/g of 235U fission) ÷ 54.0.
