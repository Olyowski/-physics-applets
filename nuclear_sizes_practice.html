Key Formulas:
Nuclear radius: r = r0 · A1/3 where r0 ≈ 1.2 × 10−15 m and A is the mass number
Nuclear diameter: d = 2r
Volume of a sphere: V = (4/3)π·r3
Density: ρ = m / V (mass of nucleus ≈ A × 1.66 × 10−27 kg)
Remember: All nuclei have nearly the same density (≈ 2.3 × 1017 kg/m³) because nucleons are packed at the same spacing regardless of A.
